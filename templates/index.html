    Know the limits

    • Route participation and targets per route are unavailable.
    • Historical weather forecasts and pregame injuries are not integrated.
    • Snap participation measures offensive plays, not routes.
    • The model can miss offseason changes in a receiver's role.
    How we calculate and test the projection

    We predict receptions, receiving yards, touchdowns, and other scoring contributions using a random forest trained on 2023–2024. We convert those estimates to full PPR. Target trends enter as features rather than an arbitrary boost.

    2025 provides the chronological validation sample. 2026 is a diagnostic replay because some outcomes were inspected earlier. Evaluation covers receivers with sufficient prior history and recorded stats or positive offensive snaps, conditional on participation.
